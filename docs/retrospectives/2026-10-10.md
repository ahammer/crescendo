quiet-window preparation and a quiet run finishing outside its window. Both acquire logs and
dashboard details call all three deployment drains. This cycle contains **1,945** such dispatch
refusals and a long sampled hold without a matching managed deployment start. The missing reason
prevents trustworthy attribution. The creator/intent of the long hold is unknown; inferring an
orphan and automatically deleting it would exceed this evidence. [L, S1, D, P]
