## Causes and changes in this PR

**Cold-start admission was the largest preventable loss.** D declares `ae7cc585` deployed at
09:22:51 UTC. L's first missing-`:avoid` crash follows six seconds later; the last is at
06:13:26 UTC, immediately before #63's deployment. Every one of the 6,224 crash dumps has
`issues_observed_at: nil` and `poll_check_in_progress: true`. Quiet demand was probing routing
using a fabricated partial throttle before receiving Governor policy. PR #63 fixes the owner,
and post-deployment dispatch/observations demonstrate recovery. The missing hours are not
evidence of zero work, continuous resource occupancy or a scheduler-weight deficit. [L, D, S1, G]

**The dashboard could conceal this missing observation.** Original aggregation removes missing
timestamps when selecting the oldest known read; original polling health trusts a configured
interval or `checking?: true`, and original tracker health omits an unobserved tracker. A fresh
Crescendo sibling therefore makes a cold MetalRain process appear observed. An older successful
read already degraded system health, but polling itself could still display healthy. The
regression reproduced healthy polling instead of the required warning before the fix. [P, L]
